Dog Creek Lodge

A 34-person Nordic ski resort lodge in Olney, Montana — tucked into the Whitefish Range, surrounded by glacier-fed meadows and old-growth pine. We have the whole place to ourselves.

The Main Lodge houses the restaurant and bar. The Guest Lodge, Event Lodge, and cabins spread across the property so everyone has space while staying close.

  • Main Lodge — restaurant, bar, great room, fireplace
  • Guest Lodge — additional sleeping rooms
  • Event Lodge — game room, bar/kitchenette
  • Cabins — private accommodation options
  • 250+ km of Nordic trails on property
  • Restaurant hours: Wed–Thurs 4–9pm, Fri–Sun 12–9pm
  • Address: 8775 US Hwy 93 N, Olney, MT 59927

What We'll Do

Activities

🌲
Nordic Hiking
Miles of trails through old-growth forest and alpine meadow right out the front door.
🎣
Fishing
Dog Creek and the nearby Stillwater River offer excellent trout fishing for all skill levels.
🦌
Wildlife Watching
Moose, deer, and birds are regular visitors to the meadow. Glacier NP is an hour away.
🔥
Bonfire & Stargazing
Zero light pollution. The Milky Way is visible on clear nights. Fires every evening.
🍕
Wood-Fired Pizza
The Lodge restaurant serves hand-crafted wood-fired pizza and Montana-sourced fare.
⛰️
Glacier Day Trip
Glacier National Park is 65 miles north. Worth a full day for those who want to explore.

Travel & Logistics

Flying In

Fly into Glacier Park International Airport (FCA) in Kalispell, MT. It's the closest major airport — about 40 miles south of the lodge. Arrive June 16 to maximize your time.
