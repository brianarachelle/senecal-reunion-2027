Where We're Staying

Dog Creek Lodge

Dog Creek Lodge sits in Olney, north of Whitefish, with enough room for the family to stay together while still having space to spread out. We’ll have the lodge and grounds to ourselves for the reunion.

The property includes the Main Lodge, Guest Lodge, Event Lodge, and cabins. Frank and Mary are coordinating rooms, meals, and the remaining details directly with the lodge.

  • Main Lodge with restaurant, bar, great room, fireplace, and commercial kitchen
  • Guest Lodge with additional rooms
  • Event Lodge with a game room and kitchenette
  • Cabins across the property
  • Breakfast at the lodge and dinners coordinated with the lodge
  • Address: 8775 US Hwy 93 N, Olney, MT 59927

Getting There

Travel & Logistics

Northwest Montana is easiest to explore by car. We’ll use RSVPs to help connect relatives who want to share a rental car or coordinate an airport pickup.

Flying In

Glacier Park International Airport (FCA) in Kalispell is the closest airport. Alaska, Delta, American, United, Allegiant, and Sun Country serve the airport, although nonstop routes vary by season.
